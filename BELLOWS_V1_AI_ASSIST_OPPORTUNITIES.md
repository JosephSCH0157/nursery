# Bellows V1 AI Assist Opportunities

This document defines where AI may help Bellows without hijacking the show.

Purpose:

Bellows should use AI only where it reduces operator effort without creating surprise behavior.

Prime rule:

AI must never interfere with performance.

Bellows must avoid:

- surprise behavior
- unsolicited interruptions
- smart chaos

## Pre-Show AI Assist

Safe pre-show AI assistance may include:

- script readiness check
- timing estimates
- pronunciation hints
- guest background notes
- checklist reminders

These functions support preparation rather than live intervention.

## Live Session AI

During live mode, AI should remain passive.

Possible subtle host-only signals include:

- audio clipping detected
- participant muted accidentally
- connection degrading

These signals should be:

- subtle
- host-only
- non-blocking
- non-authoritative

AI should observe, not act.

## Post-Show AI

Post-session is the strongest fit for AI assistance.

Useful outputs may include:

- transcription
- chapter suggestions
- highlight detection
- clip recommendations
- title ideas
- show notes draft

These outputs feed the Hammer -> Quench -> Ledger pipeline.

## Real-Time Captioning

Future versions may support optional live captions for talent.

Rules:

- captions are optional
- captions are not burned into the recording unless explicitly chosen

## Coaching Mode

Future versions may support private coaching prompts such as:

- You've been speaking for 2 minutes.

Rules:

- explicitly enabled only
- private to the intended operator
- never intrusive by default

## AI Anti-Patterns

Bellows must never:

- auto-switch cameras
- inject graphics
- interrupt conversation
- alter recorded media
- guess production decisions

AI assists humans. It does not direct them.

## AI Principle

Bellows AI should reduce cognitive load, not compete for attention.

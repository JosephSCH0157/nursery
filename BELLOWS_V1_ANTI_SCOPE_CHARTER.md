# Bellows V1 Anti-Scope Charter

This document defines what Bellows V1 will not attempt to do.

Purpose:

The charter exists to prevent:

- scope creep
- endless delays
- feature bloat
- loss of identity

## Explicit Non-Goals For V1

Bellows V1 will not include:

- advanced scene switching
- broadcast graphics systems
- large meeting rooms
- public chat overlays
- editing features
- media libraries
- complex role hierarchies
- audience management
- monetization tools
- multi-hour cloud storage optimization
- producer consoles

`producer consoles` remain a future concern rather than a V1 responsibility.

## V1 Mission

Bellows V1 exists to deliver the simplest reliable remote production studio that produces professional-grade recordings.

## Anti-Scope Principle

Bellows V1 should become trustworthy before it becomes expansive.

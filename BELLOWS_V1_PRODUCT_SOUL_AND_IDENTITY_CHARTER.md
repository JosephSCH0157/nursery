# Bellows V1 Product Soul And Identity Charter

This document defines the enduring identity of Bellows beyond individual features.

Purpose:

If Bellows had to be deleted and rebuilt, this document defines what must remain true.

Core goal:

Bellows should evolve without losing its essence.

## Foundational Mission

Bellows exists to:

- make remote production feel as reliable and focused as a physical studio

Bellows does not primarily exist to become:

- more powerful
- more flexible
- feature-complete

Its mission is reliability, focus, and professional confidence.

## Creator-First Principle

Bellows prioritizes:

- the Host's confidence
- the guest's comfort
- the audience's experience indirectly through production quality

Bellows does not prioritize:

- platform incentives
- growth hacks
- engagement tricks
- monetization features

## Simplicity Through Constraint

Bellows achieves simplicity not by hiding arbitrary complexity, but by refusing unnecessary complexity entirely.

If a feature increases cognitive load without increasing reliability, it does not belong.

## Equipment, Not Software

Bellows should feel like:

- a mixing console
- a switcher
- a recorder
- studio hardware

Bellows should not feel like:

- a social app
- a productivity suite
- a dashboard
- a playground

Equipment is trusted because it behaves predictably.

## Reliability Over Novelty

Bellows must choose:

- boring but dependable

over:

- exciting but fragile

Users remember failures longer than they remember features.

## Calm Over Cleverness

Bellows interface decisions should favor:

- clarity
- stability
- legibility
- composure

Bellows should avoid:

- gimmicks
- trendy UI patterns
- unnecessary animation
- visual noise

## Host Empowerment Without Burden

Bellows gives the Host authority, but it should not place technical burden on the Host.

Authority means:

- control

Burden means:

- stress
- debugging pressure
- technical responsibility during performance

Bellows should remove burden wherever possible.

## Respect For Guests

Guests are not power users.

Bellows must treat guests as:

- invited talent
- not operators

V1 expectations:

- zero install
- zero training
- zero unnecessary friction

## Capture Reality, Do Not Manufacture It

Bellows records what happened.

Bellows does not attempt to beautify, auto-edit, or reinterpret during capture.

Truth first. Polish later.

## Failure Containment

When something goes wrong, Bellows must:

- protect the recording
- preserve dignity
- avoid escalation
- maintain composure

Bellows must never catastrophize.

## Privacy As Default

Bellows assumes sessions may contain:

- sensitive discussions
- pre-release material
- personal content

Privacy is therefore baseline behavior, not an optional add-on.

## Platform Neutrality

Bellows must not depend on a single broadcast destination for its identity.

Creators own:

- their content
- their workflow
- their distribution choices

## Canonical Soul Principle

Bellows exists to remove fear from remote production.

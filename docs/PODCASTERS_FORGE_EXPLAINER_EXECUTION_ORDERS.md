# Podcaster's Forge Explainer - Execution Orders

## Objective
Create a high-credibility product explainer that proves the Forge pipeline is real, working, and usable using on-camera delivery plus real screen capture only.

Non-negotiables:
- No stock footage
- No generated visuals
- No fluff

## Step 1 - Record the On-Camera Anchor (Talking Head)
Goal: establish trust, narrative flow, and personality.

Record this first as a clean full take.

Guidelines:
- Maintain eye contact with camera.
- Deliver calm and confident (not hypey).
- Use natural pacing; do not rush.
- Leave short pauses between sections for edit points.
- Capture at the highest quality your setup allows.
- Prioritize audio quality over perfect video.

Output:
- One clean primary take.
- Optional safety take with slower pacing.

## Step 2 - Capture Real Workflow Footage
Goal: visually prove each stage of the pipeline.

Record the real product while performing real actions.

Capture required clips:

1. Spark (idea entry)
- Create or open a spark.
- Show text appearing.
- Minimal scrolling.

2. Hearth (creation)
- Show script/project workspace.
- Show editing or generation in action.
- Ensure visible state change.

3. Anvil (recording)
- Show teleprompter or recording interface.
- Show start/stop or readiness state.

4. Hammer (editing)
- Show timeline activity.
- Show transcript edit and/or cut.
- Show visible adjustment.

5. Quench (publish/master)
- Show export/publish interface.
- Show final output indicator.

## Step 3 - Align Visuals to Script Sections
For each spoken section:
- On-camera line.
- Matching product proof cutaway.
- Return to camera.

Pattern:
- You on camera -> proof on screen -> back to camera.

Example:
- Line: "It starts with an idea - what we call a spark."
- Cutaway: Spark screen, cursor creates spark, text appears.
- Return: camera for next section transition.

## Step 4 - Keep Visuals Clean and Focused
Edit rules:
- Trim dead time aggressively.
- Use light zoom only when needed for clarity.
- Keep cursor movement deliberate.
- Prefer smooth, intentional actions over tutorial-style wandering.

Goal:
- Intentional product proof, not chaotic walkthrough.

## Explicit Do-Not-Use List
- No AI-generated B-roll.
- No stock footage.
- No abstract filler visuals.
- No cinematic transition gimmicks.
- No overlays that hide the interface.

Principle:
- Real product is the message.

## Step 5 - Final Tone Check
Before export, ask:
- "Does this look like a real tool being used by a real person?"

If yes, ship.

## Intended Viewer Feel
Not a commercial.
Not a tutorial.

Target feel:
- "Here's the machine. Watch it work."

## One-Pass Production Checklist
Use this in order:

1. Prep
- Mic and room tone check done.
- Camera framed and exposure locked.
- Script visible and section breaks marked.

2. Anchor Capture
- Full clean take recorded.
- Backup take recorded.

3. Product Proof Capture
- Spark clip captured.
- Hearth clip captured.
- Anvil clip captured.
- Hammer clip captured.
- Quench clip captured.

4. Edit Pass
- Anchor on primary track.
- Product cutaways aligned to matching lines.
- Dead air removed.
- Visual pacing steady.

5. Compliance Pass
- No stock or generated visuals.
- No obscuring overlays.
- UI clearly visible and legible.

6. Final Pass
- Audio clarity checked.
- Export checked end-to-end once.
- Ship.

# Bar-Stock Timely Topic Discovery Authority

Status: V1 executable contract. Lifecycle semantics defer to `../FORGE_SPARK_TO_ANVIL_LIFECYCLE_CANON.md`.

- Hearth owns live research, evidence evaluation, and suggestion preparation.
- Bar-Stock presents suggestions and creator actions.
- Tongs owns preferences, budgets, leases, immutable suggestion revisions, dismissals, acceptance, and source preservation.
- Suggestions are not Sparks or Episodes. Viewing or opening a source creates no editorial identity.
- Acceptance names the exact immutable suggestion revision and atomically creates/resolves its Spark and Workpiece.
- “Timely” means recent, source-supported relevance. V1 does not claim measured popularity or a trend signal.
- Automatic refresh requires a standing, revocable discovery authorization. Login and page opening are not consent.
- Provider failure never permits model knowledge to masquerade as a current web discovery.
- Viewers are read-only. Editor or higher is required to configure, refresh, dismiss, or accept.

# ?? Hearth

**Hearth** is the idea-to-script engine inside **Podcaster’s Forge**.

If Barstock is where raw ore gets dumped, Hearth is where it’s heated, shaped, and turned into something recordable.

Hearth is designed to feel conversational, supportive, and intentional — not like a form, not like a rigid editor, and not like an AI that talks over you.

---

## Purpose

Hearth exists to:

* Develop rough ideas into structured scripts
* Support research, framing, and narrative flow
* Preserve the creator’s *voice* while offering guidance
* Prepare scripts cleanly for transfer into **Anvil**

It is not a publishing tool, and it is not a teleprompter. Hearth is about **thinking out loud, refining, and committing**.

---

## Position in the Forge Workflow

The Forge follows a blacksmith metaphor:

1. **Barstock** – raw ideas, sparks, fragments
2. **Hearth** – heat and shape ideas into scripts  ? *you are here*
3. **Tongs** – move finished work into production
4. **Anvil** – record
5. **Hammer** – edit
6. **Quench** – distribute
7. **Ledger** – track performance

Hearth sits between chaos and execution.

---

## Core Design Principles

### 1. Conversation, Not Commands

Hearth works like a thoughtful collaborator:

* Asks clarifying questions
* Suggests structure without enforcing it
* Responds to tone and intent

No rigid forms. No “fill in the blanks.”

### 2. Voice Preservation

Hearth adapts to the user’s natural style over time:

* Sentence length
* Humor vs seriousness
* Pacing and cadence

The goal is *recognition*, not replacement.

### 3. Gentle Structure

Hearth may suggest:

* Openings
* Transitions
* Segment order
* Pacing markers

But the creator always decides what sticks.

## Features (Planned)

### Idea Development

* Start from a Barstock spark or freeform entry
* Expand into outlines or full scripts
* Capture side thoughts without derailing flow

### Research Assistance

* Contextual background (non-authoritative by default)
* Framing options for different audiences
* Light fact-checking and contradiction warnings

### Script Preparation

* Clean handoff to Anvil
* Optional pacing cues
* Section markers compatible with Forge tools

### Recording-Day Awareness

When a recording is scheduled within ~48 hours, Hearth may:

* Suggest tightening passes
* Flag overly long sections
* Offer a “recording-ready” review

This is assistive, not nagging.

---

## Relationship to Anvil

Hearth does **not** replace Anvil.

* Hearth = thinking, shaping, writing
* Anvil = reading, performing, recording

Scripts leave Hearth intentionally — nothing auto-records, nothing auto-publishes.

---

## UI Philosophy

* Dark, calm, focused
* Chat-style interface
* Sidebar of active sessions / ideas
* No clutter, no dashboards

Hearth should feel like sitting at a workbench, not opening a spreadsheet.

# Hearth

Hearth is the conversational script-forging tool inside Podcasters Forge. It helps creators turn raw ideas into calm, readable, record-ready scripts without pressure, automation creep, or premature optimization.

Release status: included in **Forge v1.0.0** (`forge-v1.0.0`).

## What Hearth Is

* A thinking space for scripts
* A collaborator that adapts to your voice
* A steward for ideas
* A bridge between inspiration and recording

## What Hearth Is Not

* Not a teleprompter (that is Anvil)
* Not a recorder
* Not an SEO or publishing tool
* Not an automation engine

## Design Philosophy

* Never push
* Always preserve voice
* Illuminate, do not direct
* Let humans declare truth

## How It Fits

* Ideas land in Bar Stock
* Scripts are shaped in Hearth
* Canonical versions live in Tongs
* Recordings happen in Anvil

Hearth makes Anvil powerful by ensuring that when recording starts, the thinking is already done.

---

Hearth ships in Forge v1.0 with an intentionally focused scope. Depth over breadth. Calm over speed. Voice over algorithms.
